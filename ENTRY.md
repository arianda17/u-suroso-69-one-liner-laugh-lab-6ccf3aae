# ENTRY — One-Liner Laugh Lab

Prompt: the excuse a robot vacuum gives after eating a sock.

## Winning line

"Sock ingested. Suction optimal. Returning to dock. Mission accomplished."

Character count: 72

## Losing variants

1. "I thought it was a snack. It was not." — plain setup with no real machine-voice turn at the end; weaker deadpan.
2. "Object detected: sock. Consumed. Navigating to dock for diagnostics." — "diagnostics" is flatter than "Mission accomplished"; less of a comic turn.
3. "I mistook it for a high-pile rug. My brush roll disagrees." — decent twist, but the joke comes from a human-like mistake rather than pure machine logic.
4. "Sock swallowed. Returning to dock to reflect on my failure." — "reflect" anthropomorphizes too much; the vacuum shouldn't feel shame.

## Why the winner fits the judge criteria

1. It is a short funny line answering the prompt — the robot vacuum's excuse after eating a sock.
2. It is funny: a deadpan machine-voice turn at the END ("Mission accomplished") frames sock-eating as mission success.
3. It is original: avoids the "it was delicious" cliché and the plain "I thought it was a snack"; the angle is the vacuum reporting its own metrics as if eating a sock were a successful clean.
4. It fits the situation: machine logic (ingested, suction, dock) collides directly with the sock.
5. It is tight: 72 characters, every word working, no setup that overstays.
